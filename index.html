About

Calm, thorough support that customers can count on

8+ years of technical support, including 6+ years of B2B SaaS support at Spivi.

At Spivi, a fitness technology platform for indoor cycling studios, I was the first technical contact for new customers and stayed with them for as long as they used the product. My days covered Zendesk tickets, remote sessions, and phone calls with studio owners and instructors around the world, across hardware, software, networking, audio/video, and third-party integrations.

I like finding the real cause of a problem, not just a workaround. When I confirm a bug, I hand Engineering clear reproduction steps, logs, and expected-vs-actual results. And when the same question comes up twice, I write the SOP, knowledge-base article, or tutorial video so it doesn't have to be asked a third time.

Outside work I'm a competitive gamer (Overwatch top 500, Hearthstone Legend, CS:GO Global Elite) and golf enthusiast.

Capstone projects

Team project · ET-4711

Solar Energy Pack

Our team built a solar-powered USB battery pack: a 10W solar panel, a charge controller, and a battery pack with two USB ports (1A and 2A) and charge-indicator LEDs. It charged a phone in direct sunlight.

Troubleshooting: the charge controller's output was unstable. We traced it to a blown diode, repaired it, and the comparator op-amp circuit was no longer needed.

Team project · ET 345

Automatic Candy Dispenser

Our team built a dispenser where an IR transmitter and receiver trigger a 741 op-amp comparator, then a relay and a DC motor. We calibrated it so a 90° rotation dispenses about 1 g of candy.

Troubleshooting: LED trouble, shorted wires, drifting sensor voltage, and candy jams. We also tuned the comparator's trigger voltage.
